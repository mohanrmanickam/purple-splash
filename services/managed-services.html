Managed Services

Our managed IT services team is available 24×7×365.

Purple Splash Infonet Pvt. Ltd. offers functional, technical, and operations support for revenue recognition systems such as Zuora RevPro, and across cloud platforms including Zuora Billing. We can help you manage day-to-day revenue operations with our seasoned team of certified consultants.

Our teams choose customer collaboration over contract negotiation and go beyond to resolve the issues. All of our managed services can be independent services, or they can coexist depending on the client’s needs. Round-the-clock working in different time zones makes our team available for client support at any point.

Our multi-product managed services

Zuora RevPro support

Admin and configuration, customizations and upgrades, data extraction and validation, 606 IT support, data migration, user training, health checks, and audit assistance.

Zuora Billing support

Admin and configuration, customizations, integration with Zuora RevPro and ERPs, data migration, user training, functional and technical support, and project management.

What is included

Technical support

Root-cause analysis, data transformation, support tickets, upgrade testing, and integration using a repository of knowledge and best practices.

Functional support

Make revenue-management processes robust, flag possible non-compliance, and give solutions clients can incorporate seamlessly.

Operations support

Data collection, conversion, validation, reconciliation, revenue release, multi-currency allocations, period open/close, and disclosure reporting.

Audit support

ITGC and financial audit documentation for and around the revenue management system during implementation and after go-live.

Training

Training decks, user desktop procedures, live sessions, and a knowledge base for day-to-day referencing on the job.
