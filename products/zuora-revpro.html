Zuora RevPro

The world’s leading revenue automation solution for ASC 606 and IFRS 15.

Zuora RevPro offers customers configurable options for dual revenue books and current and future revenue guidance. It streamlines revenue automation, allowing seamless integration with any ERP. It produces consistent and accurate revenue data quickly and easily, and helps revenue teams comply with disclosure reporting requirements as per ASC 606 and IFRS 15.

Purple Splash Infonet Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted partner of Zuora. With our certified financial and technical professionals, we provide Implementation Services, Data Migration Services, and IT and Operations Support Services for Zuora RevPro.

Our years of expertise with the product and our partnership with Zuora help our clients implement revenue automation using best practices and a wide range of ready-to-build use case solutions.
