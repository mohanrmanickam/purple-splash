Zuora Billing

A single platform to manage accounts, subscriptions, and the metrics that drive a subscription business.

Zuora Billing is a single unified platform that allows you to manage customer accounts and subscriptions. You can launch new products to market quickly, scale operations, and gain full visibility into the metrics that drive your subscription business. Zuora Billing provides a single source for all customer subscription data and helps your subscription business grow in scale with each new customer.

Purple Splash Infonet Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted partner of Zuora for its Billing platform. With our certified financial and technical professionals, we provide Billing Implementation Services, Data Migration Services, and techno-functional Support Services.

In the Zuora Billing module, you can streamline and automate billing for:

  • Managing customer accounts
  • Creating and managing subscriptions
  • Launching new products to market quickly
  • Scaling billing and payment operations
  • Gaining full visibility into the metrics that drive your subscription business

Capabilities

  • Bill for any charge model
  • Automated prorations and calculations for any event
  • Hierarchical billing
  • Real-time taxation
  • Branded invoice templates

Benefits

  • Build long-term relationships with customers
  • Learn how customers use products or services
  • Calculate charges incurred from subscription changes
  • Automate invoice generation on specified schedules
  • Calculate charges based on usage

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Zuora Billing over a standard ERP billing module?

Zuora Billing is a specialized relationship-business platform designed for the complexity of subscription and usage-based models, which standard ERPs often struggle to automate.

How does Zuora Billing handle complex global tax and currency requirements?

It automates global tax compliance and supports multi-currency transactions, so your subscription business can scale internationally without manual billing intervention.

Does Zuora Billing integrate with our existing CRM and ERP?

Yes. We specialize in integration with Salesforce and back-end ERPs such as NetSuite so data flows from lead to revenue.

Can we launch new pricing models quickly with Zuora?

Yes. It is designed for rapid go-to-market strategies, allowing you to create and manage new subscriptions or update pricing models in real time.

How does Purple Splash Infonet Pvt. Ltd. ensure a successful Zuora Billing implementation?

Certified experts guide you through a systematic, end-to-end process — from requirements and design to testing and migration — with dedicated post-go-live support.
