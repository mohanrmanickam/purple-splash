About

Who Are We?

Purple Splash Infonet Pvt. Ltd. offers highly customized service with a focus on building and maintaining strong relationships with our customers. We help our clients drive business transformation in the new digital world with any part of the Revenue Automation and management process, and the Zuora Billing journey, to take the business to the next level.

We are a forward-looking company focused on delivering excellence in the form of Professional Services for Revenue Management and subscription billing. As a Professional Service Provider, we connect with all the stakeholders to understand the pain points and help address them by providing dynamic and ingenious solutions.

Our commitment to excellence is reinforced by our ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, ensuring the highest global standards for information security and data protection. This milestone reflects our dedication to maintaining a secure, resilient environment for our clients’ most critical business processes and revenue management operations.
